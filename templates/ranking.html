Priority = Activity × (1 − Toxicity)

Multiplicative composite. A compound must be both likely active and likely non-toxic to score high. High toxicity pulls the score toward zero regardless of activity — a potent-but-toxic compound ranks low.

Range: 0.0 → 1.0  ·  Best for: general-purpose triage where both axes matter equally.

Priority = Activity − Toxicity

Simple difference. Values range from −1 to +1. Compounds with more safety margin than activity earn a negative score — a useful filter for "safety-first" prioritisation.

Range: −1.0 → +1.0  ·  Best for: identifying compounds where safety margin outweighs raw potency.

Priority = Activity × (1 − Toxicity²)

Quadratic toxicity penalty. A compound that is 80% toxic gets penalised by 0.64 rather than 0.80 — moderate toxicity is tolerated more, but extreme toxicity is punished harder.

Range: 0.0 → 1.0  ·  Best for: early-stage screening where moderate tox is acceptable.

Priority = w₁ · Activity + w₂ · (1 − Toxicity)

Adjustable linear blend. Move the sliders to shift emphasis toward potency (activity) or safety. Both weights are normalised to sum to 1 — the sliders control the relative importance of each axis.

Range: 0.0 → 1.0  ·  Best for: comparing different weighting strategies side-by-side.
